# Project Domain & Context: CONTEXT.md

## 1. Project Background & Purpose

The **Agentic Multimodal Research Platform** was initiated to solve the fundamental flaw of single-prompt Large Language Models: **unverifiable factual synthesis**.

In demanding research domains—such as deep technology evaluation, legal compliance, scientific research, and intelligence analysis—standard generative AI answers cannot be relied upon because they lack full source lineage, hallucinate citations, and cannot process multimodal primary materials (scanned research papers, PDFs with complex tables, images, and live web pages) systematically.

This platform replaces ad-hoc prompting with an **autonomous agentic investigation pipeline**. It treats research as an engineering process: planning a dependency graph of sub-investigations, executing parallel evidence collection, verifying claims through an adversarial critic, grounding facts in a hybrid vector/BM25 knowledge base, and synthesizing a comprehensive report.

## 2. Key Domain Concepts & Entities

### 2.1 Research Job (`ResearchJob`)
The top-level entity representing a user's overarching investigation inquiry. It contains the primary research question, optional domain constraints, execution status (`pending`, `running`, `completed`, `failed`), and timestamps.

### 2.2 Research Task (`ResearchTask`)
An individual unit of work generated by the `PlannerAgent`. Tasks form a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) with explicit dependency resolution (`depends_on`). Workers include:
- `WebResearchAgent`: Discovers external literature and documentation.
- `DocumentAnalysisAgent`: Analyzes local files, PDFs, DOCX, and images.
- `CriticAgent`: Validates extracted evidence for accuracy and consistency.
- `ReportAgent`: Synthesizes findings into the final report.

### 2.3 Source (`Source`) & Evidence (`Evidence`)
- **Source**: A reference artifact (e.g., a URL, an uploaded document, or a literature preprint) with cryptographic content hashing (`content_hash`) to avoid duplicate ingestion.
- **Evidence**: An atomic claim extracted from a Source, paired with exact supporting quote spans (`supporting_text`), confidence ratings, and verification status (`verified`, `refuted`, `unverified`).

### 2.4 Research Report (`Report`)
The final deliverable generated upon completion of all DAG tasks. It structures the findings into:
- Executive Summary
- Methodology
- Key Findings (with per-topic confidence ratings and citations)
- Conclusions & Limitations

## 3. Operational Modes

### Mode A: Local-First Air-Gapped (Privacy Maximized)
- Runs completely on local hardware.
- Model provider: Local `Ollama` hosting `llama3.1`, `llava`, and `nomic-embed-text`.
- Vector store: Local `ChromaDB` or `InMemoryStore`.
- Web research: Restricted to local document corpus or intranet HTTP proxies.

### Mode B: Hybrid Enterprise (Performance & Scale)
- Utilizes local models for sensitive document parsing and embedding.
- Routes complex multi-step reasoning and report synthesis to official cloud providers (Google Gemini API / OpenAI-compatible endpoints) via the intelligent `ModelGateway`.
- Enforces strict SSRF security filters on all outbound web exploration.

---

## 4. Key Design Philosophies

1. **Zero Hallucination Tolerance**: If evidence cannot be directly cited to an ingested source or verified web page, it is flagged as uncertain or excluded by the Critic.
2. **Deterministic Observability**: Every model invocation, token usage, latency, and agent decision is persisted in `agent_runs` and `model_calls` tables for forensic auditability.
3. **Decoupled Modularity**: Every subsystem (AI routing, ingestion parsers, vector search, database models) exists as an independent Python package in `packages/`, ensuring testability and clean boundaries.
